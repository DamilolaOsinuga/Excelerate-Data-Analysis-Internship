## Key Findings Across All Four Weeks

### 1. Strong and Consistent Growth (2022–2024)
Application volume grew significantly year-over-year, validating strong market demand for Excelerate's programmes. 2024 data indicates continued acceleration.

### 2. Dominant Seasonal Pattern — Q1 and Q3 Peaks
Q1 (Jan–Mar) drives the highest application volumes across all years. Q3 is the secondary peak. Q2 and Q4 are consistently lower. This pattern aligns with academic calendars and is reliable for capacity planning.

### 3. 21-29 Age Group is the Core Market
The 21-29 cohort represents the largest share of applications and maintains competitive acceptance rates. However, the 16-20 segment shows engagement worth capturing through targeted programme design.

### 4. Nigeria Leads Geographic Distribution
Nigeria accounts for the largest share of applicants — consistent with Excelerate's African market presence. Significant international diversity exists, with opportunities to grow underrepresented regions.

### 5. Programme Category Drives Acceptance Rate
Acceptance rates vary significantly across programme categories. High-demand analytical and technology programmes are more selective. This creates a clear segmentation opportunity for pipeline management.

### 6. Churn Concentrated in Pending Backlog
A meaningful share of applications remain in Pending or Under Review status beyond expected processing windows. Targeted intervention at this stage represents a recoverable conversion opportunity.

---

## Strategic Recommendations Delivered

1. **Invest in Q1 and Q3 capacity** — align programme intake slots with the confirmed seasonal demand pattern
2. **Launch Q2 and Q4 activation campaigns** — targeted marketing during slow quarters can smooth demand and fill available spots
3. **Build a Pending application SLA** — set a maximum review time (e.g. 21 days) and trigger follow-up outreach to reduce churn
4. **Prioritise the 21-29 segment** while developing an entry pathway for the 16-20 cohort (pre-professional track)
5. **Deploy the predictive model** as an internal scoring tool to identify high-probability applicants and prioritise outreach
6. **Expand geographic marketing** beyond Nigeria — the data shows untapped applicant pools in underrepresented regions
